About Mandi Lens

A clean, fast, phone-friendly front-end for India's open mandi price data.

Where the data comes from

Every price on this site comes from the Open Government Data Platform of India (data.gov.in), published by the Directorate of Marketing & Inspection. It is free, public and openly licensed. We do not invent, estimate or predict a single figure.

What the numbers mean

  • Modal price — the price at which most of the day's trade actually happened. This is the number to trust.
  • Min / Max — the lowest and highest accepted bids that day, usually reflecting different quality grades.
  • Quintel — all prices are ₹ per quintal. One quintal = 100 kg.
  • Arrival date — the day the produce arrived at the market, which is not always today.

Honest limits

  • Not every market reports every day, and some markets report late.
  • A price is an indicative wholesale rate, not an offer to buy or sell.
  • Transport, commission and grading costs are not included.
  • If the data looks old, the banner at the top will say so.
